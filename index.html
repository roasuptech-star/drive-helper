Drive Helper

Drive Helper is a private internal tool. It works only with the single Google account of its owner and is not offered to the public.

What it does: makes copies of document templates in Google Drive folders of its owner, names the copies, creates folders for them and, when asked, turns on link sharing for a new copy.

It does not read, sell or share any other personal data.
